Requires a `.env` file at the repository root (`ROOT_DIR/.env`) defining at least `SECRET_KEY`; UAT/production additionally require `APP_ENV=uat|production`, `ENABLE_HTTPS_ENFORCEMENT=True`, `DEBUG=False`, a random ≥50-char `SECRET_KEY`, and non-wildcard `ALLOWED_HOSTS`. Default dev runs via `python manage.py runserver` from `src/`; migrations via `python manage.py makemigrations` / `migrate`. Production deploys behind IIS/reverse proxy with `SECURE_PROXY_SSL_HEADER = ("HTTP_X_FORWARDED_PROTO", "https")` and logs rotate to `Logs/app.log` / `Logs/error.log` (5 MB × 30 files).
